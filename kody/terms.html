Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Português

1. What the app does

Kody is a reminders app with an artificial intelligence assistant. You tell it what you need to remember, by text, voice or a photo of a document, and Kody records the reminder and alerts you.

2. The artificial intelligence can make mistakes

  • The assistant may misunderstand a date, a time or a request, and reading photos can have errors. Check important reminders, such as due dates, appointments and medicine.
  • Alerts depend on your phone: notifications and alarms must be allowed, and the device may delay alerts to save battery. Don't use Kody as the only way to remember something critical.
  • Kody doesn't give medical, legal or financial advice. It only records and reminds you of what you tell it. For medicine and treatments, always follow your doctor.

3. Who can use it

The app is intended for people aged 13 or older. Anyone under 18 should use the app with the knowledge of a parent or legal guardian.

4. Proper use

Don't use Kody for illegal purposes, to get around the free chat limits or to overload the service. We may limit or block access to the chat in case of abuse.

5. Free chats and Kody Plus

  • Features that don't use the artificial intelligence are free. Chatting with Kody includes a number of free chats per month, shown in the app, which may change.
  • Kody Plus is a subscription charged by Google Play, at the price and period shown before purchase, with the free trial shown in the store.
  • It renews automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions; access continues until the end of the period already paid. If you cancel during the free trial, you aren't charged.
  • Kody Plus has a daily fair-use limit, far above normal use, to protect the service from abuse.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

6. Availability

The chat depends on the internet and on third-party services, such as Google Gemini, and may sometimes be unavailable. Reminders, the calendar and alerts keep working offline.

7. Liability

The app is provided as is. We don't guarantee that the assistant understands every request correctly or that every alert arrives at the exact time on every device.

8. Privacy

How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
