Kody Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Português

This policy explains what data the Kody app (“the app”) uses, why, and what your rights are. The controller is Gama Vexa (55.329.617 VINICIUS SANTIAGO MONTECOCO, CNPJ 55.329.617/0001-47), based in Panorama/SP, Brazil. Contact: viniciussan25@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your phone

Your reminders (names, dates, times and notes), the conversation with Kody, the name or nickname you gave and your preferences are stored in the app's storage on your device. We have no access to them. If you uninstall the app, this data is erased from the phone.

2. Sign-up

The app has no sign-up or login and doesn't ask for your e-mail or phone number. The name or nickname is optional and is only used for Kody to greet you.

3. Chatting with the artificial intelligence

When you chat with Kody, we send the following to process the request: the text of your message, the current date and time, your name or nickname (if given), the app's language and the reminder details needed to answer. This goes through Kody's server to Google Gemini, the artificial intelligence service that writes the answer. It only happens when you use the chat: the messages on Kody's card on the home screen are put together on the phone itself.

Testing phase: while Kody is being tested, we use Gemini's free tier. On that tier, Google may use what is sent, including with human review, to improve its products. So don't type sensitive data such as passwords, card numbers, detailed health data or full documents into the chat. When we move to Gemini's paid tier, this policy will be updated.

4. Kody's server

Kody's server, hosted on Cloudflare, passes the conversation on to Gemini without storing its content. To count each month's free chats, apply limits against abuse and know who subscribes to Kody Plus, it keeps only:

  • a random code the app creates on your phone, which doesn't contain your name, e-mail, phone number or reminders;
  • how many chats and requests that code used per day and per month;
  • if you subscribe to Kody Plus, the Google Play purchase receipt and the date the subscription ends.

5. Photos of documents

The text in photos is read on the phone itself, by Google's text recognition installed on the device: the photo isn't sent to anyone. Photos taken with the app's camera are deleted right after they're read. When you use the camera inside the chat, only the text read from the photo is sent to Kody, as a message from you (see section 3).

6. Backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android keeps a copy of the app's data in your Google account, to bring it back when you install Kody again or change phones. That copy is made and protected by Google, and we have no access to it. When you tap “Save a backup”, the app creates a file with your reminders, the conversation and your preferences, which stays wherever you choose to save it. Keep that file somewhere safe and don't share it.

7. Kody Plus subscription

Payments are processed by Google Play. We don't receive your card details. We only receive the purchase confirmation and receipt from Google, to unlock Kody Plus.

8. App permissions

  • Notifications and Alarms & reminders: to remind you, at the right time.
  • Internet: to talk to the artificial intelligence through Kody's server.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Camera: opened through the phone's own camera app, only when you ask.
  • Microphone: voice typing uses Android's own speech recognition, only when you tap the microphone.

9. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share personal data for advertising.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't build profiles of you or use your reminders for any other purpose.

10. Who data is shared with

  • Google (Gemini): processes the chat messages (section 3).
  • Cloudflare: hosts Kody's server (section 4).
  • Google Play: processes Kody Plus payments (section 7).

These services may process data outside Brazil, including in the United States, under the safeguards in their contracts, as allowed by Brazil's LGPD (art. 33).

11. Legal bases

We process chat messages and the purchase receipt to provide the service you use or bought (LGPD, art. 7, V). The random code and usage count are based on our legitimate interest in offering free chats with fair limits and protecting the service from abuse (art. 7, IX).

12. How long we keep data

The conversation is not stored on the server. The usage count and the random code are kept as long as needed to apply the monthly and daily limits. The Kody Plus receipt is kept while the subscription exists.

13. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, portability or deletion of your data by writing to viniciussan25@gmail.com. In the app, you can edit or delete any reminder, clear the conversation and, in Settings › Erase all my data, remove everything from the device at once.

14. Minimum age

Kody is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you know a child has used the app, write to viniciussan25@gmail.com.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app, with the date it was updated.
