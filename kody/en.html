Kody

The planner for adult life, with AI. Tell Kody what you need to remember, by text or voice, and it writes it down and reminds you at the right time.

Português

What Kody does

  • Remembers documents, maintenance, medicine, bills and appointments: “I changed the oil today”, “my license expires in March”.
  • Routines that repeat by themselves, like daily medicine or the gym every Monday.
  • A month calendar, on-time reminders and a daily summary of what's coming up.
  • Reads documents from photos: the reading happens on the phone itself.
  • Works in English and Portuguese, with a backup of your data.

Free and Kody Plus

Everything that doesn't use the AI is free: reminders, routines, calendar, alerts and backup. Chatting with Kody includes a few free chats a month, and Kody Plus unlocks unlimited chats.
